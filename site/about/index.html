About Making Human Safe

Purpose: before you go anywhere or do anything, at home, on the street, at the beach or abroad, see what has actually happened there, what is happening now, and what keeps you and your family safe. Every fact has a date and a source.

How we check facts

  1. Collect public information: official alerts and statistics, news reports, and reports from people.
  2. Check every fact. Each incident keeps its source link and a sentence copied word for word from it. Plain code confirms that sentence is really in the source before anything is published. AI may help read news; it never decides what is true.
  3. Connect facts to places, spots, causes, seasons and the people they affect.
  4. Show only what affects you, with the date it was last checked.

We never name victims. We never call a place "safe"; we say what is known. When we get something wrong, we correct it openly.

Disclaimer

Making Human Safe gathers public information (official alerts and statistics, news reports and reports from visitors) and shows it with its source and the date it was last checked. It may be incomplete, out of date or wrong, and conditions can change in minutes. The absence of incidents or alerts does not mean a place or activity is safe. Always follow official warnings, lifeguards, flags and local authorities. This site does not give medical, legal or emergency advice. Reports from visitors are labelled as reports until confirmed. We are not an emergency service: in danger, call your local emergency number.

Privacy

We do not use tracking cookies or advertising trackers. MakeUsSafe (coming soon) will work inside your browser, so we will not see your family details. We will store contact details only if you ask for alerts, and you will be able to delete them anytime.
