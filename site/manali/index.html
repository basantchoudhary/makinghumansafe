Before you go

Taxis

The only official figure we found is the Rohtang Pass permit: ₹500 permit fee plus ₹50 congestion charge per car, booked on the Himachal government portal. Manali taxi fares are set by the local taxi union, not the government, and we found the union card only as reproduced by a travel site; news reports describe drivers charging up to four times the standard rate, especially in snow. Book the Rohtang permit yourself on the official portal and ask to see the union rate card before agreeing a taxi fare.

Water sports (rafting, parasailing, boats, coracles)

1 reported incident since 2025, 1 death. Latest: 2025-01-07.

Where: Raison paragliding site

  • Use only licensed operators with a visible registration; ask for it.
  • Always wear a life jacket and a helmet for rafting, and check the harness and straps yourself before parasailing.
  • Don't go out in rough sea or strong wind, even if the operator says it's fine.
  • Agree the price and duration before you start.

Waterfalls and treks

No checked incidents in our records yet.

  • Don't swim or climb on rocks near waterfalls, especially after rain: water rises fast and rocks are slippery.
  • Check whether the area is open; many falls close in the monsoon.
  • Go with a guide or a group, carry water, and tell someone your plan.
  • Know how far the nearest hospital is (see Help nearby).

Scooters, bikes and roads

3 reported incidents since 2025, 9 deaths. Latest: 2026-07.

Where: Atal Tunnel north portal, Banjar, Rani nallah, Manali-Rohtang road

  • Wear a helmet, every ride; it's the law and it saves lives.
  • Carry your driving licence; rent only from shops that ask for it.
  • Photograph the scooter before you take it, to avoid false damage claims.
  • Don't ride after drinking or on unlit roads at night.

By private taxi

  • If hiring a private taxi, ensure that the driver has experience driving in hilly regions and does not have the habit of drinking alcohol. Also make sure the driver has done the trip at least once before and knows all roads. Also make sure that the driver is used to drive for long hours. Many operators turn off air-conditioning during the majority of the hill journey, or demand to be paid extra if asked to. Best to confirm this prior to booking. If you are going to Manali through Chandigarh-Ropar-Kiratpur-Bilaspur-Mandi-Kullu highway, then your driver would need to drive on hilly roads for at least 6 hours. The expected price is somewhere around ₹3000 to ₹4000/day for an Indica hatchback or a bigger Innova car from Delhi. To get the best deals on cabs, look for cabs that came one-way from Manali/Kullu to drop tourists at Delhi. These cabs (usually with HP01K registration plates) offer 60% cheaper fares, as they've been paid fully by the previous customer. Also, you can find them at parking station near Majnu ka tilla/ New Aruna Nagar.

Stay safe

  • Manali is a safe city but during seasonal time a surge of visitors keep visiting this beautiful place and some transients exploit this opportunity for their own benefits. Best be exercise caution if in a crowd.
  • If you get lost and don't have enough directions get around the city then just ask for Mall Road (Manali town centre). From there you can easily head to any destination of your choice.
  • Don't exchange currency in illegal market. Always ask for receipt after doing currency trade with exchange dealer. There is a Western Union office on the Mall Road.
  • A word of caution about paragliding in Manali. Many pilots of these parachutes are un-trained and obtain licences in the usual way any licence is obtained in India. Many a tourist visits the Lady Willingdon Mission Hospital with broken bones, sometimes protruding out, after accidents. Some para-gliders crash into innocent bystanders who end up with spinal injuries and paralysis. Serious, complicated cases with multiple organ injuries and heavy blood loss are stabilized to the extent possible and referred to either Chandigarh or Shimla. Sadly, some don't make it. Blood availability is very very limited in this hilly area. So think very carefully before deciding to paraglide in Manali.
  • Women should avoid going into small lanes and by-lanes after it is dark and make sure that you are not wearing any jewellery while walking on the streets. There were two reported rapes of tourists in 2018 alone.

If you are travelling with…

Women

  • Share your live location with someone you trust when travelling at night.
  • Use the prepaid taxi counter or a booked cab; note the vehicle number before getting in.
  • Stay on lit, busy roads after dark; avoid empty beach stretches at night.
  • Call 112 in an emergency or 181 for the women's helpline.

Teens

  • Don't swim after drinking, at river mouths or outside the safe season.
  • Go in groups and tell someone where you're going and when you'll be back.
  • Wear a helmet on rented scooters.

Kids

  • Swim only at beaches with lifeguards, and only in flagged areas.
  • Keep children within arm's reach in the water.
  • Carry water and avoid the midday sun.

Elderly

  • Know the nearest hospital to where you stay (see Help nearby).
  • Carry a list of medicines and an emergency contact.
  • Be wary of calls claiming to be police or officials asking for money: hang up and call 112.
