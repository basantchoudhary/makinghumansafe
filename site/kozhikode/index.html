Who's going?

  • Children only in knee-deep water, with an adult within arm's reach the whole time.
  • A life jacket for anyone who can't swim well, even in shallow water.
  • Stay away from rocks, river mouths and the calm-looking gaps between waves (rip currents).
  • Nearest hospital: CHC Melady, 0.1 km from Thikkodi (Kallakath Beach area).
  • Most drownings in our records are groups of young friends. One person stays out of the water and watches.
  • No swimming after drinking, and never at dusk or night.
  • If a friend is pulled out, don't swim after them: shout for help, call 112, throw anything that floats.
  • Swim only where other people are around, in daylight.
  • Tell someone where you are going and when you'll be back.
  • Isolated coves and rocks are where help is furthest away.
  • Waves can knock you over even in knee-deep water: stay at the edge.
  • Wet rocks and steps are slippery: wear grippy footwear and use a hand to steady yourself.
  • Carry your medicines and an emergency contact card.
  • Nearest hospital: CHC Melady, 0.1 km from Thikkodi (Kallakath Beach area).

Activities: what has happened, and what to do

Incidents are counted only when the source article was opened and found to name the place and the event (0 of 0 stored incidents passed this check). Many incidents are never reported.

Swimming (sea, rivers and ghats)

No checked incidents in our records yet.

  • Swim only where lifeguards are on duty and inside the flagged area; a red flag means no swimming.
  • Rivers can look calm and still pull hard below the surface: at ghats, stay on the steps or hold the chains.
  • Don't swim after drinking, at night, or alone.
  • If caught in a current, don't fight it: swim parallel to the shore, then back in.
  • Keep children within arm's reach in the water.

General advice.

Waterfalls and treks

No checked incidents in our records yet.

  • Don't swim or climb on rocks near waterfalls, especially after rain: water rises fast and rocks are slippery.
  • Check whether the area is open; many falls close in the monsoon.
  • Go with a guide or a group, carry water, and tell someone your plan.
  • Know how far the nearest hospital is (see Help nearby).

Get around

  • Buses charge ₹8.00 for 5 km.
  • City buses are green-coloured here and they are very punctual and cheap. All the city buses originate and terminate in the Mananchira Pond area. Buses going to Pavangad, Elathur, Nadakkavu areas originate from the Head Post Office side of Mananchira. Buses going to Medical College and Mavoor Road stop at the Income Tax side of Mananchira. Buses going to Feroke, Kallayi and Beypore stop at the Girls School side of Mananchira.
  • Buses are very infrequent to the beach area. You have to walk the 2-km distance or take an auto-rickshaw.
  • Private buses originate from the 11.25817375.7863851 New Bus Stand on Mavoor Road (1 km). Private buses to nearby villages originate from the old Bus Stand at Palayam (1 km).
  • Government buses originate from the 11.25925775.7824522 KSRTC Bus Station on Mavoor road. This modern bus station has traveller amenities. Parking is provided for 270 cars. They have slow-moving, low-floor, air-conditioned buses to nearby towns. You can make use of these buses for travelling inside the city.
  • Bus numbers for green city buses:
  • Karuvan Thuruthy River. 213
  • The usual charge is ₹30 for every km.
  • Taxis, ☏ +91 495 2306023. Air conditioned taxis are available at ₹1,000 for a day. Look for them at the Railway Station or Mananchira area. (updated Mar 2020)
  • Because the weather is hot throughout the year and the humidity quite high, an air conditioned vehicle is more pleasant here. Uber- and Ola-like call taxis are also available.
  • There are many rental companies offering cars with drivers or self-drive. Traffic blocks exceeding one hour are common. Keep away from Kallayi Road and Wayanad Road as traffic is heavy and dead-slow.
  • There are not many sidewalks in town, still it is possible to walk almost everywhere near the roadway. There is a 11.24862775.780223 bridge with a sidewalk north of the train station. And there is a 11.242275.7833964 bridge without sidewalks south of the train station. On the latter is just enough space to walk between the solid line of the road and the railing. It is better to walk on the right side here. The long beach road from Beypore to Kothi Bridge is a peaceful area to walk.

If you are travelling with…

Women

  • Share your live location with someone you trust when travelling at night.
  • Use the prepaid taxi counter or a booked cab; note the vehicle number before getting in.
  • Stay on lit, busy roads after dark; avoid empty beach stretches at night.
  • Call 112 in an emergency or 181 for the women's helpline.

Teens

  • Don't swim after drinking, at river mouths or outside the safe season.
  • Go in groups and tell someone where you're going and when you'll be back.
  • Wear a helmet on rented scooters.

Kids

  • Swim only at beaches with lifeguards, and only in flagged areas.
  • Keep children within arm's reach in the water.
  • Carry water and avoid the midday sun.

Elderly

  • Know the nearest hospital to where you stay (see Help nearby).
  • Carry a list of medicines and an emergency contact.
  • Be wary of calls claiming to be police or officials asking for money: hang up and call 112.
