Official black spots on the way

What is a black spot? A stretch of road of about 500 m where, over three years, there were at least 5 crashes in which someone died or was badly injured, or at least 10 deaths (the road ministry's rule). Slow down, stay in your lane and watch for vehicles merging or crossing. The list only shows where crashes already happened often; a road without black spots is not proven safe.

  • km 0.0: Konappana Agrahara, Hosur mainroad. (Electronic city)
  • km 1.5: NICE ROAD NAIDU PROFARTIES (Electronic city)
  • km 8.8: Near T John College, NICE Road (added 2025, reported; location approximate) newer list, reported by Deccan Herald
  • km 13.9: Nice road Poorvankara Apartment (Thalaghatapura)

Before you leave

  • Pick a co-pilot. The driver never touches the phone; the co-pilot navigates and reports hazards in Google Maps.
  • Tyres: check pressure and look for cracks; tyre age is the 4-digit code on the side (week and year).
  • Lights: dip your high beam for oncoming traffic and when following a car. If you are dazzled, look at the left edge of the road and slow down.
  • Speed and lanes: keep left except to overtake, leave a long gap, and expect two-wheelers and late lane changes near exits.
  • At night: watch for unlit parked vehicles on the shoulder. Sleepy? Stop. A 20-minute nap beats an accident.
  • Seat belts for everyone, front and back, and a helmet on every two-wheeler rider and pillion.
